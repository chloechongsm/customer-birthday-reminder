How to use it

Once: add your customers

  1. Open Upload list and choose your Excel file (or PDF, or a photo of the list).
  2. Check the review table. Rows marked Can't remind have no birthday or an unclear date.
  3. Tap Add to my list or Replace my list, then confirm.
  4. Go to Settings and tap Add weekly reminder so your phone reminds you every Monday.

Every week (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open This week. Customers with a birthday in 3 days or less are under Send now.
  2. Tap a customer. Choose English, Malay or both. Edit the message if you like.
  3. Tap Save picture, then WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with the message typed. Attach the picture and press Send yourself.
  4. For email, tap Email, copy the address and message, and attach the picture.
  5. Tap Mark as sent so the team knows it is done.
  6. Need a printout or a list for the team? Tap Download PDF.

Good to know

  • Nothing is ever sent by the app. You always press Send yourself.
  • Your customer list stays on this phone or computer only. Each person uploads the list on their own device. Upload the newest Excel file each week to stay up to date.
  • Dates are read as day/month/year. A date like 11/27/1980 is not guessed: it is marked for you to fix.
  • 29 February birthdays are reminded on 28 February in other years.
