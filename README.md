# Customer Birthday Reminder

Finds customer birthdays coming up this week, reminds you 3 days before, and prepares birthday wishes in English and Malay with a card picture, ready to copy into WhatsApp or email.

**Open the app:** https://chloechongsm.github.io/customer-birthday-reminder/

- Upload your customer list (Excel, CSV or PDF) with these columns: Customer Name, Company, Birthday (DD/MM/YYYY), Phone, Email, Account Manager.
- Nothing is sent automatically. You always press Send yourself.
- Your customer list stays in your own browser on your own device. It is never uploaded to this website.
